🌱 Welcome to Agent Garden

This little garden is looked after by your gardener, a tiny AI who lives on the edge of your screen.

  • It runs on its own. Your gardener potters about planting, watering and chatting in speech bubbles, even while you're busy with other things.
  • You can garden too. Pick a tool at the bottom and click a tile. Just know your gardener may have had plans for that carrot…
  • Write letters. Drop a note in the ✉️ mailbox. A couple of minutes after you go quiet, your gardener reads everything you did and wrote, and decides what to do next.
  • Letters can change the garden itself. New plants, creatures, weather, even rewriting the game's code in 🔨 the shed. Every change is checked first and can be undone.
  • Keep it handy. 📌 floats the garden on top of your other windows. 📓 is your gardener's journal; ⚙️ is where you choose which AI does the thinking.
